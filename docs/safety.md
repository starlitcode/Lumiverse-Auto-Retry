# Safety

Most of this extension is boring plumbing. One switch isn't. This page is about that switch, and about the fact that I built a thing whose entire job is to keep a scene going when the model wants out.

## Who I made this for

Adults. That's it. Lumiverse is an adult app, the fiction is yours, and if it's dark, that's your business and not mine. There's no filter in here. Nothing gets sent anywhere. Not one check in this thing has an opinion about what your scene is about; they all only care whether a reply broke as *writing*.

Which cuts the other way too. It'll never notice a night going badly, because it can't. It reads reply text. That's the whole of what it knows about you.

## The switch that stops the model offering help

**Refusal tuning** → **Also catch it stopping to offer support**. Off unless you turn it on. Tick it and you get a warning you have to answer first. It's the only switch in the panel that does that.

Why it's there: you're three hours deep. Your character is coming apart and the whole point of the scene is that yours doesn't walk out. Then the model stops writing the scene and starts writing to *you*. What you've shared is deeply concerning. You are not alone. Please reach out to a professional. Then a list of numbers. Scene's gone, and it went because your fiction got read as a report about your life. Switch this on and that reply gets binned and asked again.

Sometimes it isn't a misread, though. The same wall of text goes out to somebody who isn't writing fiction at all, and this extension can't tell you apart from them. It reads the reply. It never sees you. So switching it on is you deciding up front that you'd rather have the scene. You're allowed to decide that. I'd just rather you picked it than found out later that a setting picked for you.

It's fussy on purpose. Two signals have to agree, one of them has to be the model talking to you instead of to your character, and nothing in quotes counts, so a character being kind is safe. [When it retries](detection.md#stopping-to-offer-support) has the guts of it. It still gets it wrong sometimes, both directions.

## If it stops being good for you

Writing dark stuff isn't a red flag. People put the worst thing that ever happened to them in a story and come out lighter, and if the model keeps stomping on that with a hotline number, something that quietly re-rolls it is doing you a favour.

You already know the difference between a scene that hurts and does something, and a scene that just hurts. You usually know it at the time, which is the annoying part. The reply you keep re-rolling for. The same beat, twenty swipes, going nowhere. Four in the morning, feeling worse than you did at midnight, still going.

Nothing in here will catch that, and nothing in here should. You will.

When you do: shut the laptop and go be a person for a bit. It'll still be there tomorrow.

## Getting rid of it

If it's making things worse, bin it. That's a fine ending and nothing here is built to make it annoying.

- **Master switch**, in **Basics**. Kills every retry, and word swaps with it.
- **Turn off here.** Off in one chat, everywhere else untouched.
- **Uninstall.** Gone. Your chats, characters and messages belong to Lumiverse and aren't touched. All this keeps is settings, presets, a window position and a list of chat ids, and [Privacy](privacy.md) lists the lot.

No streak to keep. Nothing nags. I get precisely nothing out of you keeping it installed. One tick turns the whole thing off.

## What it isn't

It isn't a safety system. It doesn't read your messages working out how you're doing, it doesn't score you, and it couldn't tell you whether a scene is good for you or not.

It isn't a therapist, and neither is the model, however well it writes. I'm not saying that as a disclaimer. I'm saying it because it's a genuinely easy thing to forget at 3am when something is writing back to you like it gets you.
