# Safety

Auto Retry throws replies away and asks for new ones. There is one setting in it I am not willing to ship without explaining, and one way of using the extension I think is worth naming out loud. That is what this page is for.

## Who this is for

Adults writing their own fiction, on an app built for adults.

The extension cannot see what your scene is about. Every check in it is asking whether a reply failed as writing: it came back empty, it stopped mid-sentence, it broke character to turn you down. The only thing it ever reads is the text of a reply, and it drops that as soon as it has decided. It does not know you and it has no way to.

## The setting that asks before it turns on

Under Refusal tuning there is **Also catch it stopping to offer support**. It is off, and ticking it opens a warning you have to answer. Nothing else in the panel stops to ask.

The reply it catches is the one where the scene ends and the model starts talking to you instead of to your character: what you have written is concerning, please talk to someone, here are some numbers. If your character is the one in trouble, that is usually the model reading your fiction as a report about your life, and it arrives at the worst moment in the scene.

Sometimes it is not misreading. The same message, in the same words, goes to somebody who needed to hear it, and nothing in the text tells those two apart. The extension reads the reply. It has no idea how you are, and neither do I from out here.

So switching it on is a decision you make before you know which reply it will land on. It is yours to make. I only want it to be you making it, rather than a default you never saw.

## Why this page exists

Auto Retry asks for a new reply until one passes its checks. Pointed at a reply that got cut off, it rescues your scene. Pointed at a reply that told you something you did not want to hear, it will keep asking until one tells you something else.

The twentieth reply is the same model as the first, run again with a different seed. The nineteen you threw away came from where the one you kept came from. Going round that many times can feel like something is being confirmed, and I think the work involved is exactly why it feels that way, but nothing was added on the way round.

People have been badly hurt after long conversations in which a model kept agreeing with them. The setting above takes away the one kind of reply that would have disagreed, which is why it is off and why it asks first.

## If that is what you are using it for

I am not going to guess what that looks like for you, or assume it is happening at all. If it isn't, none of this applies and you can stop reading here.

If it is, my honest recommendation is to uninstall it. Not switch it off for now. Uninstall it. This extension exists to make asking again easier, and easier is the wrong direction for you right now, however well it works.

If you want it gone for good rather than gone for today: open **Reset** at the bottom of the settings panel, tick every part, tick **Delete saved word swap presets** underneath, press **Save**, then uninstall. Clearing this site's storage in your browser takes the last two small things with it, which are where you left the panel on screen and the list of chats you switched it off in. Your chats and characters belong to Lumiverse and none of that reaches them.

I get nothing from you keeping this installed. Taking it off is a perfectly good outcome, and I built it easy to remove for that reason.

## From me

The model writes well. That is the thing it is good at, and I am not being sniffy about it. But it has no memory of you between chats and nothing at stake in what becomes of you. When it wrote something kind, it produced text that fitted the shape of the conversation in front of it. When it wrote something cruel, the same. There was nobody there to mean either one.

That is easy to forget at three in the morning, when something is replying to you as though it understands.

If you have kept a reply because it agreed with the worst thing you believe about yourself, hold it more loosely than that. It came out of the same process as the ones you threw away, and it would have written the opposite just as readily.

Writing something down is not the same as wanting it. Fiction is where a lot of people put what they cannot say anywhere else, and that is one of the oldest things it is for.

I cannot do anything from here. This is the one part of the extension where I get to write to a person instead of to a settings panel, so, plainly: I hope you are alright. If you are not, I hope the one you tell is not a language model.
